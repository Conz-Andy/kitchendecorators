Frequently Asked Questions

What locations do you serve?
We cover the whole of Southern England.
What surfaces can be spray painted?
The majority of manufactured finishes can be spray painted — varnished wood for sure, melamine and most previously painted surfaces. We offer test patches to verify adhesion. We don't spray custom raw unpainted MDF doors.
What paints are used — are they special?
High-quality furniture paint is used; some call them designer paints. We use Mylands Eggshell, Little Greene Intelligent Eggshell and Intelligent Satinwood, and Farrow & Ball's Modern Eggshell. All are water-based with low emissions and an extensive choice of colours.
What's the process?
Removable doors and drawer fronts are collected and sprayed at our workshop, then returned and reinstalled. Fixed components receive dust-free sanding before careful in-situ finishing. For a small to medium-sized kitchen our time in your home is usually no more than two to three afternoons.
How do I choose a colour?
Select your colour from the paint manufacturer's website — Little Greene, Dulux, Farrow & Ball or Edward Bulmer — or browse our kitchen gallery for inspiration. A free colour consultation is included.
Do you spray paint the kitchen fixtures in my home?
Yes, depending on accessibility. Fixed panels are masked and sprayed; awkwardly positioned fixtures are hand-painted with fine rollers for a matching finish.
Can I change the handles?
Yes. The handles are removed for spray painting, so it's the perfect time to change them too.
How long before I can use the kitchen?
Your kitchen furniture will be dry enough to handle and use within a day of painting, with refitting within the week. Full curing takes several weeks.
What if the paint gets scratched or marked?
Fully cured paint will resist all but the hardest contact. Retouching is done with the original paint, brush-applied.
Do you paint radiators and household doors?
Yes we do — we paint radiators and regular doors with the same durable finishes, throughout your home.
