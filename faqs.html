Frequently Asked Questions

What locations do you serve?
We cover the whole of Southern England.
What surfaces can be spray painted?
The majority of manufactured finishes can be spray painted — varnished wood, melamine, MDF and most previously painted surfaces.
What paints are used — are they special?
We use a specialist paint designed specifically for kitchens, which colour matches to all the major brands — Dulux, Farrow & Ball, Little Greene and Edward Bulmer. Water-based, low emissions and an extensive choice of colours.
What's the process?
Removable doors and drawer fronts are collected and sprayed at our workshop, then returned and reinstalled. Fixed components receive dust-free sanding before careful in-situ finishing. Our time in your home is just 2 afternoons.
How do I choose a colour?
Select your colour from the paint manufacturer's website — Little Greene, Dulux, Farrow & Ball or Edward Bulmer — or browse our kitchen gallery for inspiration. A free colour consultation is included.
Do you spray paint the kitchen fixtures in my home?
Yes, depending on accessibility. Fixed panels are masked and sprayed in place for a perfectly matching finish.
Can I change the handles?
Yes. The handles are removed for spray painting, so it's the perfect time to change them too.
How long before I can use the kitchen?
You can use your kitchen the same day. Full curing takes a few weeks, so just treat the surfaces gently at first.
What if the paint gets scratched or marked?
Fully cured paint will resist all but the hardest contact. If anything does get damaged, we will always return to remedy it free of charge — unless you're a serial offender, then we charge!
Do you paint radiators and household doors?
Yes we do — we paint radiators and regular doors with the same durable finishes, throughout your home.
