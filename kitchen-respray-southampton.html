Kitchen Respray Southampton: Your Questions Answered

Do you cover Southampton and the surrounding villages?
Yes. We cover the whole city, from Ocean Village to Shirley and Bitterne — SO14 to SO19. Your doors and drawer fronts are collected and sprayed at our workshop near Botley, so our time in your Southampton home is just 2 afternoons.
How long does a kitchen respray take in Southampton?
About 5 days from start to finish, with only 2 afternoons of disruption in your home — and you can use your kitchen the same day. Here’s how the week works.
How much does a kitchen respray cost in Southampton?
It depends mainly on the number of doors and drawer fronts, the door style and any extras such as an island. A respray typically costs up to 75% less than a new kitchen, and quotes are free with no obligation. Our kitchen respray price guide explains what affects the price.
Can you match the colour I’ve chosen?
Yes. Our specialist kitchen paint is colour matched to Dulux, Farrow & Ball, Little Greene and Edward Bulmer, or to any swatch you give us. Recent colours around Southampton include Farrow & Ball Hague Blue and Farrow & Ball Wimborne White. A free colour consultation is included with every quote.
Can my kitchen be resprayed?
Most manufactured finishes can be — varnished wood, melamine, MDF and most previously painted surfaces. We’ll confirm when we see your kitchen. See which kitchens can be resprayed.
What if the paint gets marked after you’ve finished?
Fully cured paint resists all but the hardest knocks. If anything does get damaged, we come back and put it right free of charge.
