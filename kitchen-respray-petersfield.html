How much does a kitchen respray cost in Petersfield?
It depends mainly on the number of doors and drawer fronts, the door style and any extras such as an island. A respray typically costs up to 75% less than a new kitchen, and quotes are free with no obligation. Our kitchen respray price guide explains what affects the price.

Fully cured paint resists all but the hardest knocks. If anything does get damaged, we come back and put it right free of charge.
