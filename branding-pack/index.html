Brand guidelines

The rules that keep everything looking like one company — share these with printers, signwriters and anyone making something for the business.

Name

The Kitchen Decorating Co

The trading name used on workwear, cards, quotes, invoices and the Google Business listing.

kitchendecorators.co.uk

The website address — always written in lower case, without “www”.

Kitchen Respray Specialists

The strapline. Set in capitals with wide letter spacing, beneath or beside the name.

Logo

Cube logo — full colour

Download SVG

On dark backgrounds

Place the logo on a white panel so the navy face of the cube stays visible.

Clear space

Keep empty space around the logo of at least a quarter of its height on every side.

Minimum size

No smaller than 15 mm tall in print, or 40 px tall on screen.

Don’t

Stretch, rotate, recolour, add shadows or outlines, or redraw the logo — always use the SVG file supplied.

Colours

Navy — primary#2F3191 · RGB 47 49 145

Red — accent#BD1F2E · RGB 189 31 46

Teal — accent#14AEA9 · RGB 20 174 169

Ink — text#1C1D2E · RGB 28 29 46

Navy leads; red and teal are used in small amounts, most often together as the red · navy · teal stripe. For print, ask the printer to colour-match to these values and approve a proof.

Typography

Bricolage Grotesque

Headings, the company name and phone numbers. Extra Bold.

Figtree

Everything else — body text, details and labels. Regular to Bold.

Free to use

Both fonts are free from Google Fonts, so printers and designers can download them.

Printing notes

  • Items in [square brackets] are placeholders to fill in before printing.
  • To make a PDF: open a design, press Save as PDF / Print, choose “Save as PDF”, and make sure “Background graphics” is ticked and margins are set to “None”. Chrome gives the most accurate result.
  • Business card printers usually add a 3 mm bleed in their upload tool — upload the PDF and check their preview.
  • For T-shirts, send the printer the SVG logo along with the mockup so they can match placement and size.
  • Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (headings) and Figtree (text), both free from Google Fonts.
